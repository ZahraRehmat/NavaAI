Privacy / 02

Privacy, plainly.

What NAVA AI reads locally, what stays on your PC, and where third-party app policies still apply.

Effective 15 November 2026Local-first preview

Local by design

The bundled speech and punctuation models run on your Windows computer. NAVA AI is not a hosted chatbot and does not send ordinary microphone audio to a NAVA server.

When enabled, microphone input is used for wake phrases, commands, and dictation. You can turn NAVA AI off or disable the microphone from its controls.

Microphone and screen context

Some controls use screenshots, Windows accessibility metadata, OCR, or browser accessibility information to locate visible targets. These inputs are used to perform the requested local action.

Local files and logs

The app may create local settings, app catalogs, diagnostic logs, cached browser data, and user-selected recordings or downloads. These remain on the computer unless you choose to copy or share them. Remove sensitive data before sharing diagnostics.

Websites and third-party apps

When you ask NAVA AI to open a website or use an application, that application’s own privacy policy applies. NAVA AI does not control how a third-party service processes information you enter there.

Your choices

  • Turn NAVA AI off or disable its microphone.
  • Remove sign-in startup from Windows settings or the installer shortcut.
  • Delete local logs and cached data using normal Windows file management.

Contact

Questions and privacy concerns can be raised through github.com/ZahraRehmat/NavaAI. This preview policy may be revised as the project develops.
