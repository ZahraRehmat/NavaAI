Terms and conditions.

The straightforward rules for using the NAVA AI preview installer and Windows assistant responsibly.

Effective 15 November 2026Preview release

About NAVA AI

NAVA AI combines offline speech recognition, native Windows controls, smart-grid labels, and direct application integrations to make everyday computer tasks more reachable by voice.

NAVA AI is preview software. It may contain incomplete integrations or compatibility limits. Keep normal backups and do not depend on it for safety-critical, financial, medical, or emergency tasks.

Permitted use

  • Use NAVA AI only on computers you own or are authorized to control.
  • Respect the terms, privacy rules, and security requirements of applications and websites you use.
  • Do not use NAVA AI to bypass authentication, access protected systems, or control another person’s computer without authorization.

Commands, installation, and updates

Voice commands can be misheard and a target can be ambiguous. Review visible choices before selecting them. You remain responsible for confirming destructive, sensitive, or externally visible actions.

The installer creates a per-user installation and may add desktop or sign-in startup shortcuts when selected. Updates can change command coverage, so review release notes and retain the published checksum.
